# VQA material issue tracker

A single-page issue tracker that works directly with an Excel file on your own computer.

**Open it:** https://pann22222.github.io/CA-vqa-tracker/ (Chrome or Edge on a computer)

## How it works
- Click **Choose tracker folder** and pick the folder that holds your tracker Excel (a workbook with a `VQA Issues` sheet).
- Every save is written straight back into that Excel. Photos go to a `photos` folder, PowerPoint exports to `reports`, and a backup of the Excel goes to `backups` the first time you save each session.
- Dropdown options (customers, suppliers, models, statuses…) come from the `Choices` sheet in your Excel.

## Privacy
The page runs entirely in your browser. It does not upload anything: your Excel, photos and reports stay in the folder you pick.
**Never commit tracker data (Excel files, photos, reports) to this repository.**
